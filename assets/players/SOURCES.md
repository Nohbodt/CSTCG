# Player portrait sources and edits

Alpha 0.6 uses 25 AI-assisted edits of identified real-player source photos. The image-editing tool removed backgrounds and team/sponsor marks, produced head-and-shoulders PNG cutouts with true alpha transparency, and replaced jerseys with plain rarity colors. These are source-based image edits, not unaltered photographs or a promise of pixel-identical faces. Their likenesses are reviewed against their recorded sources.

Jersey palette: Common graphite `#343844`; Rare cobalt `#185AC6`; Epic violet `#713BD6`; Legendary champagne `#BC965B`. Source photos remain available through their pinned distribution URLs and their hashes are preserved separately from the edited asset hashes in `portrait_sources.json`. Godot imports the full-resolution PNGs at up to 768 pixels with mipmaps. Runtime assets are local and never fetched during play.

Original photographic rights remain with the original photographers/owners. Repository code licenses and another project owner’s permission statements are not treated as photography licenses. The original profile-photo sources record HLTV credits without naming individual photographers; the broky source carries a PGL watermark. Background removal and clothing changes do not replace the original credit.

No accepted source photo is installed for syrsoN, RUSH, ALEX (Alex McMeekin), interz or F1KU; they retain monograms. The different professional named alex found in a candidate collection was excluded.
