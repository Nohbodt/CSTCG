# Series 1 design validation

The completed design kit was checked at its authoring boundary. These checks do not represent a new playable game release.

- Exactly 60 distinct player identities and 90 distinct card definitions: 60 originals, ten Elite Showcases and twenty Beta Event editions.
- Four pools with exactly 50 unique definitions each and ten designs for every assigned role; 200 total memberships.
- Every advertised card is reachable under its product's actual slot table. Every nonzero rarity/finish selection has an eligible pool.
- Every slot sums to exactly 10,000 basis points. Per-card slot probabilities each sum to unit probability across that slot; expected-copy counts sum to the opening size.
- Every alternate edition matches its base profile's eight natural values and policy. No artwork finish adds a stat bonus.
- Alpha and Bravo share exactly forty definitions, with identical prices, rarity counts and odds.
- Every proposed purchasable product's expected direct release return is below its price. Full market and combat balance remain future validation.
- The PDF has 51 pages, contains all ninety global collector numbers, and keeps every extracted text bound inside its page. The final card-register page was visually reviewed.
- Twenty real Chromium/Playwright browser checks passed: all four pool sizes, role/rarity/finish filtering, search, eight-attribute inspection, closing and restored focus, empty-state feedback, reset, mobile overflow, and absence of page-script errors.
- Desktop, modal and 390-pixel mobile layouts were captured and visually reviewed. The managed browser used a standard loopback HTTP preview because it blocks file URLs; no browser or network policy was changed.

The source of truth for exact labels and values is the manifest and its vector text layers. Generated illustrations establish material/composition direction, rather than supplying verified photographic assets. Runtime catalogue installation, legacy inventory migration, detailed trait behavior and real-player portrait production are not claimed by these design checks.
